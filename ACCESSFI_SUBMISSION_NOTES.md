## 250-500 Word Project Summary

AccessFi is a deposit-backed premium access platform for crypto-native communities, research clubs, private cohorts, and gated digital products. Today, most premium communities still rely on fragmented tools like Telegram, Discord roles, Notion pages, and manual billing. That creates poor onboarding, weak access control, limited proof of who should have access, and no unified member account.

AccessFi turns premium access into a programmable financial primitive. Creators publish a vault with a teaser, protected assets, and an access policy such as deposit-to-unlock, subscription access, token-gated access, or allowlist access. Members join through a low-friction account flow and get one account view showing joined vaults, downloadable assets, proof links, and reserve-aware membership state. Judges and operators get a dedicated proof console that ties storage refs, access policy, membership status, checkout state, and unlock history together.

The product is built with Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and Supabase for auth and persistence. It is architected around sponsor integrations: Flow is used for consumer-facing wallet and payment rails, Lit Protocol is used for encrypted access control and programmable permissions, Storacha/Filecoin is used as the decentralized storage path for protected assets, and NEAR is wired for intent-style abstraction and future settlement flows. The MVP already ships a working public vault page, member dashboard, gated asset download flow, and vault-specific proof console.

This is an `Existing Code` submission. The hackathon work added a substantial new product surface focused on premium access, programmable rights, and proof-linked member flows. We are targeting the `Infrastructure & Digital Rights` and `Crypto` tracks. Integrated sponsor bounties: `Flow`, `Lit Protocol`, `Storacha/Filecoin`, and `NEAR`.
